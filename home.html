About

Design that has to work on a Tuesday, not just in a review.

I started out doing print identity work, which taught me that a good system has to survive being reproduced badly by someone in a hurry. That's shaped how I approach product design since — fewer precious details, more decisions that hold up when three other people are also touching the file.

Lately I split my time between early-stage product work and identity projects for small studios and makers, usually for teams shipping their first real design system.

  • Brand identity

    Naming, mark, typography and the guidelines that keep it consistent after I've left the project.

  • Product design

    Research-backed UX and interfaces for web and mobile, built on systems your engineers will actually keep using.

  • Motion & interaction

    Small, purposeful animation — the kind that explains a state change instead of decorating one.
